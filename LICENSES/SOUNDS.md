# Sounds – sources & licenses (Spider Drop)

All sounds are recorded samples released under **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No synth beeps. Most were reused from earlier games in this Halloween series; the music bed, fire, whoosh, boo and wood thuds are new (trimmed, EQ'd, loudness-normalized). `tools/build.mjs` embeds them as base64 so the game works via `file://`. Credit is not required for CC0 and is given voluntarily.
